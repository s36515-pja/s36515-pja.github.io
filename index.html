The Street 27

Street 27 is a little bit of a legend. Few people believe in it's existence and even fewer visited it in person.
The localistation of the street is unknown, but the belief is that it's omnipresent. Seemingly anyone from around the world
is able to happen upon it regardless of where they are, with the only known condition being on a street in a town or a city.
The trigger to the encounter is unknown, yet unlikely to be random. Wether the person is actively looking for the street or
has prior knowledge of it's existence, doesn't seem to affect the chances to find it. There's no way to confirm
if self-proclaimed wintesses are credible, none of them were able to keep anything they found on the street.
Any injuries or marks also dissapear once they leave the street.

The witnesses all have disparate recollections of the street's appearance aside from two things:
on the street there's always a Vending machine not associated with any company and a suspiciously nonchalant girl waiting beside it
